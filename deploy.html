Deploy Wire Office

Three contracts on Robinhood Chain, in order. The wallet that deploys the office becomes the guardian: it can cancel a pending binding and propose an oracle after a 48 hour notice. It can never touch a box or its dollars, and there is no pause anywhere

  1. Deploy the box implementation, every handle's box is a clone of it
  2. Deploy the burner, it receives the 5% office fee
  3. Deploy the office with the oracle address that matches WIRE_ORACLE_KEY in Vercel
  4. Put the office and the burner into config.js and push
  5. After $WIRE launches on Pons, set it on the burner, once and for good

Every handle's delivery address depends on the office address, so redeploying the office moves every address. Deploy it once

1 · Box implementation

2 · Burner

Holds the office fee until $WIRE exists, then buys it and sends it to dead. The deploying wallet can set the token once and nothing else

3 · Office

The oracle signs handle proofs. Use the address of the key you put in Vercel as WIRE_ORACLE_KEY, never a wallet you trade from
