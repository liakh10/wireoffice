Address

Read the delivery address of a handle. It is derived from the handle, identical for everyone and cannot be swapped.

02

Point

Launch on Pons with that address as the creator fee recipient. Any launcher, any coin, no permission from us.

03

Wire

Anyone can run a box: it sweeps the curve, claims the escrow, keeps 5% for the $WIRE burn and turns the rest into USDG.

04

Claim

The handle posts a code, the oracle signs it, and after 48 hours the dollars go to that wallet, now and on every later wire.
